Inside an inference snap

{% elif slot == "description" %}

Engines are the heart of inference snaps. They manage input, generation, and output of information. Inference snaps include engines optimized for a range of hardware, and during installation, the most suitable engine is automatically chosen for the host system. Inference snaps achieve this through engine manifests and the engine manager.
