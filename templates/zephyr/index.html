Exceed the requirements of the Cyber Resilience Act

{%- endif -%} {%- if slot == 'list_item_description_1' -%}

Upstream Zephyr offers 5 years of support. However, embedded devices are often designed for longer lifecycles. Zephyr LTS bridges the gap and offers up to 15 years of patching and maintenance, exceeding the CRA mandate and freeing your teams up to focus on delivering for customers – not compliance.

Native OTA, with Golioth

{%- endif -%} {%- if slot == 'list_item_description_2' -%}

Zephyr LTS provides you with a built-in pipeline, to deliver patches and updates, through the Golioth SDK: an out-of-the-box solution for turnkey over-the-air (OTA) updates, unique credential provisioning, and remote device management. In a single command, you have everything you need to satisfy CRA requirements in an easier way, without building custom tools.

Launch sandboxed development environments

{%- endif -%} {%- if slot == 'list_item_description_3' -%}

Avoid custom configurations and vendor-locked extensions. Canonical Workshop provides repeatable, sandboxed development environments for Zephyr LTS in a single command. It's agent-ready and containerized, isn't locked to any specific hardware vendor, and lets developers run west build immediately, cutting the time from first install to first build.

One provider for Linux and MCU-class devices

{%- endif -%} {%- if slot == 'list_item_description_4' -%}

Most connected products aren't just one device, they're a fleet, with Linux-class gateways and edge compute running alongside microcontroller-class sensors and actuators. Moreover, hybrid architectures based on MPU and MCU combined in one SoC are becoming more and more present in the market.

As part of Ubuntu Pro for Devices, Zephyr LTS enables manufacturers to source support for both MCU-class and Linux-class devices from a single, trusted provider.

That means one point of contact, one procurement process, and one trusted supplier standing behind every device in your product line, regardless of which OS or RTOS it runs.
